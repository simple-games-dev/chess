How to play

Standard chess rules, including castling, en passant and pawn promotion. Pick a time control before the game starts — either a preset or your own minutes-plus-increment. Each side's clock only runs on their own turn; increment is added the moment you complete a move. Running out of time loses the game, same as checkmate, resignation, stalemate, or an automatically detected draw (repetition, insufficient material, or the 50-move rule).
